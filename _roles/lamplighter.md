## Design notes

A protective-feeling information role that doesn't actually protect anyone.
It rewards guessing the Demon's target and pairs well with scripts that have
several killing characters (Godfather, Gossip, Assassin).

## Open questions

- Should drunk/poisoned Lamplighters see a false character, or nothing?
- Too strong alongside a single-Demon, no-extra-kill script?
